Information

About the project

The problem

Teachers track assignments by hand in registers and Excel sheets. It is slow, records get scattered, and nothing is private.

The aim

A simple login-based website where a teacher manages assignments and files in one place, quickly and securely.

Secure login

Every teacher has their own account. Nobody else can see their assignments or files.

Online file storage

Assignment files are stored safely online and can be opened from any phone or laptop.

User guide

How to use this portal

  1. RegisterOpen “Register / Login”, choose Register, and enter your name, email and a password (at least 6 characters).
  2. Log inUse the same email and password. Your dashboard opens straight away.
  3. Add an assignmentType the title, pick the due date, and attach a file if you want (up to 10 MB). Press Add.
  4. Track your listAssignments are sorted by due date and overdue ones are marked. Use “Open file” to view an attached file.
  5. Log outPress Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
Good to know: your account and assignments are saved online, so you can log in from any device. Only you can see your own records.
